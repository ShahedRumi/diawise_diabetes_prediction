RESEARCH GAPS

Four questions answered by Model 2

These questions directly guide the laboratory-feature experimental design.

  1. How effective is classification without HbA1c and blood glucose?
  2. How much do accuracy and diabetic recall decrease when laboratory features are removed?
  3. What is the potential for non-laboratory screening with fewer features?
  4. How do SHAP feature importance and the recall/precision trade-off change?
Research limitations to keep in mind

Dataset metrics are retrospective results, not clinical validation. PIMA data has population constraints; the 6-feature model has substantial false-positive burden; and the primarily author-curated food workbook contains an audit of 104 estimated nutrient values. Food categories do not adapt to individual medications, allergies, or daily carbohydrate targets.
